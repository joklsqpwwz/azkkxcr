Puma Roma 24

Tag Heuer Formula 1 Solargraph Solar Quartz

Tag’s solar-powered timepiece looks like it belongs on the pit wall, and performs like it too. The all-black dial and carbon bezel give it serious F1 energy, but it’s the sun-powered movement that sets it apart. No winding, just set it and go. Plus, you can take it from training to dinner afterwards, no sweat.

Ray-Ban Mega Wayfarer

Leave it to A$AP Rocky to make Ray-Ban’s marquee sunglasses even cooler. The black edition dials up the attitude with oversized frames and blacked-out lenses that stylishly block glare. They still feel lightweight and classic, but there’s a little more edge here, making it perfect for guys who want their shades to do more than just blend in.

Untuckit CottonTek Orsini Shirt

This looks like your traditional dress shirt, but feels more like a performance T-shirt. That’s because of the brand’s cotton-nylon-elastane fabric that wicks away sweat, holds its shape, and has a hint of stretch to move with you. Of course, the shorter hem is meant to be untucked for a polished look that doesn’t look stiff. Perfect for the office, traveling, and everywhere in between.

Brooks Chariot

First launched in the ‘80s as a serious running shoe, the Chariot is now a lifestyle staple with retro appeal. The suede and mesh upper feels classic without going full dad-core, and the cushioning holds up whether you’re walking the city or sprinting through errands.

Redwing Weekender Chukka

Red Wing stripped down its classic work boot to create a lighter, more flexible chukka. The soft leather molds to your foot quickly, and the cushioned sole makes it easy to wear for hours. You still get the durability and craftsmanship the brand’s known for, but in a more casual shoe.

Reebok HYMNE Shorts

These aren’t your average mesh gym shorts. The seven-inch inseam still hits above the knee for most guys, the fit is roomy but polished enough to wear for casual fits, and the fabric has enough stretch to get you through any workout. You can train in them, but they look great enough to wear for your weekend errands.

Sperry x J.Crew Original Two-eye Boat Shoes

Leave it to J.Crew to bring boat shoes back. This time with better materials and way more style. The All-American brand’s collab with Sperry swaps out the boat shoe’s classic leather for a roughout suede, giving the preppy staple a fresh, textured finish. It still has the grippy sole and rawhide laces, but feels more elevated than anything you wore in college.

Todd Snyder Italian Linen Wythe Trouser

This trouser nails the wide-legged look without veering into clown pants territory. The cut is relaxed, but still clean, and the linen feels breezy without looking wrinkled or cheap. Naturally, it’s from Todd Snyder, so the tailoring is sharp, the fabric’s premium, and it’ll make everything else in your closet look like it needs to catch up.

Homage New York Yankees Cap Logo '68 Lightweight Hoodie

You don’t have to be a die-hard fan to rep the most iconic MLB logo. This Yankees hoodie is soft, and lightweight, and broken-in from day one. It’s great for morning walks with the dogs, or late-night games (even if you’re just watching for the vibes). It’s an easy way to show up looking like you know who’s leading the AL East.

Reiss Monaco T-Shirt

British brand Reiss linked up with McLaren to create a sharp F1 fit, blending clean lines with a cozy fabric. The look is relaxed without looking sloppy, and the Monaco logo will have you ready for your Riviera summer.
