Smoothing the Lower Face: Is PRP Better Than Dermal Filler?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

PRP (platelet-rich plasma) therapy uses your own blood to stimulate gradual collagen and elastin production, delivering natural-looking smoothing over 9–18 months across multiple sessions. Dermal fillers, by contrast, provide immediate volume correction lasting 6–18 months with minimal downtime and predictable results.

Explanation

Lower face aging often shows up as marionette lines—the vertical creases that run from the corners of your mouth down toward your chin. Many people seek non-surgical treatments to restore a smoother, more youthful appearance. Two popular options are platelet-rich plasma (PRP) injections and dermal fillers. Here's what you need to know about PRP for marionette lines versus traditional fillers.

Understanding Marionette Lines

Appearance: Deep creases framing the mouth, sometimes extending toward the jawline

Causes: Natural loss of collagen and elastin, sun damage, genetics, repetitive facial movements

Concerns: Can make you look older, tired, or sad even when you feel fine

What Is PRP?

Platelet-rich plasma is a treatment that uses your own blood to stimulate natural tissue repair.

Blood draw: A small vial of your blood is taken.

Centrifugation: The blood is spun at high speed to isolate the platelets.

Procedure Overview

PRP for Marionette Lines

Preparation: Blood draw and processing (15–30 minutes)

Treatment: Multiple small injections into the marionette lines (15–30 minutes)

Sessions: Often recommended as a series of 3 treatments spaced 4–6 weeks apart

Dermal Filler

Preparation: Topical numbing or nerve block (optional)

Treatment: Direct injections into lines or folds (15–30 minutes)

Sessions: Single session; touch-ups as needed

Combining PRP and Dermal Fillers

Many providers now offer combined treatments—using PRP to boost collagen and fillers to provide immediate volume. This "vampire lift" or "liquid facelift" can deliver both instant smoothing and long-term rejuvenation.

Aftercare Tips

Avoid strenuous exercise, heat exposure, and alcohol for 24–48 hours

Apply ice packs gently to reduce swelling

Follow your provider's instructions on topical products and massage

Final Thoughts

PRP offers a natural, regenerative approach—ideal for those who want their own healing power to do the work.

Dermal fillers deliver immediate, noticeable results—great for a quick fix.

Combining both can yield the best of each world.

Always choose a qualified, experienced provider and discuss your medical history, expectations, and any potential contraindications. And remember, if you suspect anything serious or life-threatening—chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe allergic reaction—seek emergency care right away. For non-urgent questions, speak to your doctor to determine which treatment best suits your goals and health.
