Explanation

Weight gain in the stomach area can be influenced by several factors. One key factor is stress, which leads to the release of a hormone called cortisol. Cortisol can cause the body to store more fat in the belly area. This is because cortisol affects how the body uses and stores energy, often leading to more fat being stored around the abdomen.

Another factor is biological differences. According to research, men tend to accumulate more fat in the abdominal area compared to women. This is related to differences in how fat is distributed in the body, which can be influenced by hormones and genetics.
