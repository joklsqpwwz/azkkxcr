Explanation

Sinus headaches are typically characterized by a constant, deep pain in the cheekbones, forehead, or bridge of the nose. This pain often worsens with sudden head movements or straining. Unlike other headaches, sinus headaches are usually accompanied by nasal symptoms such as congestion, runny nose, and facial swelling.

It's important to note that sinus headaches are often misdiagnosed, as many people who believe they have sinus headaches may actually have migraines. Proper diagnosis is crucial for effective treatment. If you experience persistent or severe headaches, consulting a healthcare professional is recommended for appropriate diagnosis and management.
