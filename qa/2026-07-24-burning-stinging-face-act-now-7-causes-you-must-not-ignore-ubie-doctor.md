Explanation

A burning and stinging sensation on your face can happen for different reasons. One possible cause is abnormal sweating patterns, which can be linked to itching and discomfort on the skin. Another reason might be trigeminal small-fiber sensory neuropathy, a condition where small nerve fibers are affected, leading to burning pain. Lastly, cutaneous sensory disorder may lead to unusual sensations on the skin, including burning and stinging sensations.

Here are some potential causes:

Abnormal Sweating Patterns: Sometimes, when you sweat more or less than usual, it can make your skin feel itchy or uncomfortable.
