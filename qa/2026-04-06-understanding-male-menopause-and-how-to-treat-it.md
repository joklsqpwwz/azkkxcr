# Understanding Male Menopause and How to Treat It

Reviewed by: Dr. Hal and Gameday's Medical Advisory Board

Getting older can cause men to experience physical, emotional, and psychological changes. Similar to how women experience menopause as they age, men can have mood swings, fatigue, decreased sexual drive, loss of muscle mass, weight gain, and many other symptoms. These symptoms and changes are known as “manopause.”In this article, you’ll learn more about the symptoms of manopause and the treatments available to get you back in the game.

What is Manopause?

Manopause can cause significant disturbances to your quality of life. While manopause isn’t an official medical term, it’s a popular phrase for describing the wide variety of changes that can occur in men as they get older. These changes in mood, sex drive, and more typically begin around age 50 or later. The causes are due to:

Declining Testosterone Levels: Testosterone levels tend to decrease with age in some men, leading to sexual dysfunction, changes in mood, and a decrease in energy levels.

Aging: As mentioned, the process of aging itself can cause various physical and psychological changes in men.

Lifestyle Factors: Stress, not enough sleep, and a poor diet can all lead to symptoms associated with manopause.

Medical Conditions: Some medical conditions such as obesity, diabetes, and heart disease can all influence a man’s health, potentially leading to manopause symptoms.
