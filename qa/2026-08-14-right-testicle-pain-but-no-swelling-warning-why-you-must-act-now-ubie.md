Right testicle pain without swelling is common and can occur even when the testicle looks completely normal. Causes may include muscle strain, minor injury, referred pain from the abdomen or groin, nerve irritation, kidney stones, or early-stage conditions like epididymitis or varicocele. Because the testicle is highly sensitive, discomfort can appear before any visible signs like swelling, redness, or lumps develop.

Pain without swelling should not be ignored, especially if it is sudden, severe, or persistent, as some causes (like testicular torsion) require urgent care. Understanding the possible reasons behind your symptoms is the first step toward getting the right treatment.
