Is Chikungunya virus usually fatal?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Chikungunya virus is rarely fatal, but it can cause serious complications, particularly in newborns, older adults, and people with underlying health conditions like diabetes, heart disease, or high blood pressure. While most people recover within a week, some experience long-lasting joint pain, chronic fatigue, or neurological issues that can persist for months or even years.
