What is a creatinine blood test?

A creatinine blood test determines if your kidneys are working properly. It does this by measuring the amount of creatinine found in your blood. Your body uses a waste product of creatinine for energy production; the kidneys filter creatinine out of your blood. Elevated creatinine levels in your blood indicate that your kidneys are not operating at peak efficiency. Depending on how high the numbers are, it could be an indication of impaired kidney function or even disease.

Creatinine blood tests are a great way to identify potential issues with your kidneys and give doctors the clarity to address them before they can cause any long-term damage.

Who should get a creatinine blood test

If you are experiencing any of the symptoms mentioned above or are advised by your medical professional, a creatinine blood test would be a good idea. It’s important to pay close attention to your body and not wait too long after experiencing symptoms. According to the Mayo Clinic, because kidneys are so good at working even when under crisis, symptoms may not appear until irreversible damage has already occurred. Creatinine blood tests can serve as a convenient preventative measure for early detection.
