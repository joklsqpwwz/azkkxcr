What are the potential causes of severe pain in the inner thigh?

Reviewed by Courtney Bloomer, DO

Emergency Medicine

Severe pain in the inner thigh can be caused by obturator nerve entrapment or issues with pelvic stability. These conditions may lead to discomfort and require medical attention.

Explanation

Severe pain in the inner thigh can be troublesome and may arise from different causes. One potential cause is obturator nerve entrapment, which is rare but can affect athletes. This condition involves the nerve that runs through the pelvis and into the thigh, becoming compressed or irritated, leading to pain in the groin and inner thigh area.

Severe inner thigh pain can also be caused by a femoral or obturator hernia and requires immediate medical attention.

Another potential cause is chronic anterior pelvic instability. This condition occurs when the bones in the pelvis do not stay in their proper place, which can lead to discomfort and pain in the thigh region. This instability can be due to injuries or other underlying conditions affecting the pelvic area.
