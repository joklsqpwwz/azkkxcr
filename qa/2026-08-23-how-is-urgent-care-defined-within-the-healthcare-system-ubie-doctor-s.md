Internal Medicine

Urgent care is a walk-in medical service designed for people who need prompt attention for non-life-threatening conditions but can't wait for a regular doctor's appointment. It bridges the gap between primary care and the emergency room, offering faster access for issues like minor injuries, infections, or sudden illnesses.

However, urgent care isn't always the right choice. Key factors to consider include the severity of your symptoms, cost differences versus primary care or the ER, wait times, available on-site services (like X-rays or lab testing), insurance acceptance, and whether follow-up care will be needed. Choosing the wrong level of care can mean unnecessary expenses or delayed treatment.
