Explanation

Bilateral migraines can cause headaches on both sides of the head. Migraines often only affect one side of the head can sometimes affect both sides of the head. Migraine pain often has a pulsating or throbbing quality. Migraines often are accompamined by nausea, vomiting, and/or sensitivity to bright light, loud sound, or strong odors.

Some Treatment Options For Migraines

Actions To Try During a Migraine: Napping or resting with eyes closed in a dark, quiet room can help reduce symptoms. Placing an ice pack or cool cloth on the forehead can also help.

Medications: Some medications can ease symptoms after a migraine headache starts. Other medications can be taken every day (even when you do not have headaches) to prevent future migraines.
