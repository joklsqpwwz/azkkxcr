# Is Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo (BPPV) a permanent condition, or can it be resolved? | Ubie Doctor's Note

Is Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo (BPPV) a permanent condition, or can it be resolved?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo (BPPV) is usually not permanent. In most cases, BPPV can be successfully treated with simple repositioning maneuvers, such as the Epley maneuver, which resolve symptoms quickly. However, some people may experience recurrent episodes over time, requiring repeat treatment.
