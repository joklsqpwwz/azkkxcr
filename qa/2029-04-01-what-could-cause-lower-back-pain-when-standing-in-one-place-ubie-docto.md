# What could cause lower back pain when standing in one place? | Ubie Doctor's Note

What could cause lower back pain when standing in one place?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Lower back pain when standing is often caused by prolonged standing, poor posture, or muscle fatigue. Shifting your weight, taking movement breaks, and using a footrest to elevate one foot can help reduce strain on your lower spine and ease discomfort.

However, standing-related back pain can also signal underlying issues like disc problems, spinal stenosis, or muscle imbalances that need attention. Since causes vary widely, the smartest next step is to identify what's actually driving your pain. Explanation

Standing still for a long time can make your lower back hurt. This is because your back muscles have to work hard to keep you standing.

Causes of Pain:

The load on your lower back can increase when you stand in one place for too long. This can make your muscles tired and cause pain.

Using a footrest can help make your back feel better. It changes how your weight is spread, which can reduce the strain on your back.

Changing your standing position can also help. Different positions can make your muscles work differently, which might reduce pain.
