Yes, cirrhosis of the liver can cause pain. Liver pain from cirrhosis is typically felt in the upper right side of the abdomen, just below the ribs, and may range from a dull ache to a sharp, throbbing sensation. Pain can occur because the liver becomes swollen, scarred, or enlarged, stretching the surrounding tissue and nearby nerves. Some people also experience discomfort in the right shoulder or back due to referred pain.

Pain intensity often depends on the stage of cirrhosis and whether complications like fluid buildup (ascites), infection, or an enlarged spleen are present. However, liver pain alone isn't enough to confirm cirrhosis, as many other conditions can cause similar symptoms.

Because liver-related pain can overlap with many other health issues, it's important to identify what's really going on before it worsens.
