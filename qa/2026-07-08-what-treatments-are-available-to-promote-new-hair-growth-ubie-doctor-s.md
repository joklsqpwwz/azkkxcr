Explanation

There are several treatments available to help promote new hair growth. One well-known treatment is minoxidil, which is a topical solution that can be applied to the scalp. According to research, it has been effective in promoting hair growth. Another option is rosemary oil, which is a natural treatment that has shown promise as well. Studies have compared rosemary oil to minoxidil and found that it can also help with hair growth.
