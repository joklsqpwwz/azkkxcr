# SP 2.0 - How it works

[SP How](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

Your gut microbiome report covers 94 markers across seven categories. Beneficial bacteria like Akkermansia muciniphila, Bifidobacterium, and Lactobacillus. Opportunistic bacteria including H. pylori, C. difficile, and pathogenic E. coli. Fungi and yeasts like Candida albicans. Parasites such as Blastocystis. Diversity and composition scores including the Shannon Diversity Index and species richness. Functional metabolic pathways like butyrate capacity and short-chain fatty acid production. And microbial vitamin production - your gut bacteria's ability to produce Biotin, Folate, B12, and Vitamin K2.
