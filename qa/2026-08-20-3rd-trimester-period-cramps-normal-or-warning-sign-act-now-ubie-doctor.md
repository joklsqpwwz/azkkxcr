Is it normal to experience period-like cramps during the third trimester of pregnancy?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Cramps that feel like period pain are common in the third trimester of pregnancy. Most often, these sensations are caused by Braxton Hicks contractions—irregular "practice" tightenings of the uterus. Other common causes include the baby's growth stretching ligaments, pelvic pressure, constipation, or gas. However, painful cramps paired with bleeding, fluid leakage, regular contractions, or reduced fetal movement can signal preterm labor or another complication and warrant immediate medical attention.

Explanation

During the third trimester of pregnancy, many women experience cramping sensations that are similar to period cramps. These are often due to Braxton Hicks contractions, which are a normal part of pregnancy.

What are Braxton Hicks Contractions?

Practice Contractions: These are sometimes called 'practice contractions' because they help the body prepare for labor, but they do not mean that labor is starting.
