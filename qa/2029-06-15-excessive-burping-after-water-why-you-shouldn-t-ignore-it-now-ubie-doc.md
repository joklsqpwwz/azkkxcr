Excessive burping after drinking water is often caused by swallowing air (aerophagia), changes in stomach acidity, or how the esophagus functions. Carbonated water, drinking too quickly, or underlying conditions like acid reflux (GERD), hiatal hernia, or gastritis can also trigger frequent burping. In most cases, it's harmless, but persistent or painful burping may signal a digestive issue worth investigating.

Explanation

Drinking water can cause burping for a few reasons. When you drink water, it can change the pH level in your stomach. This means it makes the stomach less acidic for a short time. This change might cause muscle tissue at the top of the stomach, called the lower esophageal sphincter, to relax. When the sphincter relaxers, it can let air escape, which might cause burping.
