Explanation

Your nails might hurt suddenly because of tiny injuries or repeated pressure that you may not always notice. For example, banging your finger or bumping your nail can cause minor damage to the nail bed, sometimes causing pain or small blood collections under the nail. In other cases, an infection like paronychia can start when tiny cuts or breaks in the skin let germs in. This infection makes the area around the nail sore and swollen. Another possible cause is nail deformities, which can sometimes make the nail more susceptible to infection. To help prevent random nail pain, try the following steps:
