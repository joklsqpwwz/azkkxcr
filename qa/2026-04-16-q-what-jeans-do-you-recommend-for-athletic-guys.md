# Q: What Jeans Do You Recommend for Athletic Guys?

Dear Men’s Health Style,

I recently lost weight and need to buy smaller jeans. I found straight cuts to be too tight through the thigh. Are there any straight cuts that are more forgiving for an athletic guy like me? I'm a little sick of feeling like I have a parachute around my calves!

Thanks,

John

Follow @MensHealthStyle on Twitter!

Dear John,

Congrats on the recent weight loss. We get questions about athletic body types pretty often, and we’re sticklers on thinking that a great fit of jeans is crucial to your overall style. So, you’ve come to the right place.

Skinny jeans should be reserved for teens or Fall Out Boy members, but straight leg can work on nearly everyone else. Kudos for pushing for a more slender cut. A straight leg can be a modern and more stylish alternative to boot cut, which flares slightly at the ankle and is best left in the ‘90s.

For an athletic type like you, go for a straight leg in a relaxed fit that will give you a little extra room in the seat and thighs. The classic style sits naturally on the waist for a comfortable and sleek silhouette. Once you find your perfect pair, keep them intact for years with our denim treating tips.

Here are three options to check out:

1. Mavi Matt Relaxed Straight Leg Jeans For under $100, these jeans from Mavi are a great bet. The cotton mixes with elastane to add just the right amount of stretch to keep things comfortable where you need it.

2. Earnest Sewn Fuller Straight Leg Showcase the hours you’ve logged at the gym with these from Earnest Sewn. We love the hand-sanded details for a slightly distressed look that still comes off as natural.

3. Seven for All Mankind Austyn Relaxed Straight Leg These jeans have a relaxed fit on top to fit and flatter athletic thighs, and a trimmer leg below the knee that stays clear of skin-tight territory. Gray has lost its drab reputation, and we love washed gray as an alternative to blue jeans.
