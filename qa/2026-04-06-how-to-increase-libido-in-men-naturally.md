# How to Increase Libido in Men Naturally

Reviewed by: Dr. Hal and Gameday's Medical Advisory Board

Experiencing low libido can be frustrating and concerning. You might notice a decrease in your sex drive, difficulty maintaining arousal, or just not feeling as interested in intimacy as you used to. While these signs can be troubling for men, it’s important to remember that you’re not alone. Millions of men experience changes in libido at some point in their lives.

Low libido is often linked to low testosterone levels, but it can also stem from lifestyle factors, stress, or medical conditions. The good news is that there are proven ways to increase libido in men, from natural changes to advanced treatments like testosterone replacement therapy (TRT).

This guide breaks down the most common causes of low libido and the best steps you can take to restore sexual health and overall well-being.

Why Men Experience Low Libido

A decrease in sex drive doesn’t happen for just one reason. In many cases, it’s a combination of physical, hormonal, and emotional factors. Common causes of low libido include:

Low testosterone levels – Testosterone is the hormone most closely tied to male libido, muscle mass, and energy. Declining levels can reduce sex drive.

Chronic stress – High cortisol levels caused by high stress can lower testosterone and make it difficult to feel aroused.

Lack of sleep – Poor sleep interferes with hormone production and overall energy.

Poor diet and obesity – Carrying excess weight and eating nutrient-poor foods can suppress testosterone production.

Alcohol or drug use – Heavy drinking and certain medications (such as some blood pressure medications or antidepressants) can interfere with libido.

Depression or anxiety – Mental health struggles directly affect sexual interest and performance.

Identifying what’s driving your symptoms is the first step toward recovery and can help determine what treatment option is best for your specific situation.

Testosterone is the primary male sex hormone, and it influences much more than just sexual function. When levels drop, men can experience a variety of symptoms that have a negative impact on their day-to-day lives. These may include:

Reduced libido and sexual performance

Loss of muscle mass and strength

Fatigue or low energy

Irritability or mood changes

Difficulty concentrating

Increased body fat

These symptoms of low testosterone can overlap with other health issues, which is why a testosterone blood test is so important for accurate diagnosis. If your provider finds that your testosterone is below the normal range, treatment may help restore your vitality and sex drive.

Natural Ways to Boost Sex Drive

Before considering medical treatment to treat low libido, making certain lifestyle changes may help address the issue. These healthy habits can naturally boost testosterone, improve body composition, and reduce stress, all potential factors in decreased libido.

Exercise regularly – Strength training and high-intensity workouts naturally raise testosterone.

Prioritize sleep – Aim for 7–9 hours of quality rest to allow your body to regulate hormones.

Eat a nutrient-rich diet – Foods high in zinc, vitamin D, and healthy fats (such as lean meats, nuts, and avocados) support testosterone production.

Manage stress – Activities like meditation, yoga, or simply setting aside downtime can reduce cortisol and improve libido.

Limit alcohol – Excessive drinking lowers testosterone and makes sexual performance more difficult.

These steps help lay a strong foundation for sexual health, but for men with clinically low testosterone, medical treatment may be necessary. That’s where TRT can come into play.
