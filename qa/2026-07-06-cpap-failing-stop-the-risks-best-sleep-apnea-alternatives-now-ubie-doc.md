Explanation

When CPAP therapy doesn't work for sleep apnea, there are several other treatments to consider. These alternatives can help improve breathing during sleep.

1. Oral Appliances:

These are like mouthguards that help keep the airway open by moving the jaw forward.

2. Positional Therapy:

This involves changing your sleeping position to reduce airway blockage, like sleeping on your side instead of your back.
