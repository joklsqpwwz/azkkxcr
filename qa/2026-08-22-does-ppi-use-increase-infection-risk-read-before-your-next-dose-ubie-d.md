Internal Medicine

Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) can increase the risk of infections, particularly in the gastrointestinal tract. By reducing stomach acid, PPIs weaken a key defense against harmful bacteria, making infections like C. difficile, Salmonella, and pneumonia more likely. Long-term or high-dose use raises this risk further, especially in older adults or those with weakened immune systems.

If you're taking a PPI and experiencing symptoms like persistent diarrhea, stomach pain, fever, or unusual fatigue, it's important not to ignore them. These could signal an infection that needs prompt attention. Understanding your symptoms early can help you make informed decisions about your health and whether to consult a doctor.
