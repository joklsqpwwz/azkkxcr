Falling asleep at work can be tough, especially if you work night shifts or have a health condition that makes you feel tired, like a sleep disorder. Here are some strategies that might help you stay awake:

Regular Sleep Schedule: Try to go to bed and wake up at the same time every day. This helps your body get used to a routine.

Short Breaks: Taking short breaks during work can help you stay alert. A quick walk or stretching can make a big difference.

Avoid Late Caffeine: Drinking coffee or tea too late can make it hard to sleep later. Try to avoid caffeine a few hours before bedtime.

Get Sunlight: If you can, get some sunlight during the day. Natural light helps your body know when it's time to be awake.

Shift Work Tips: If you work at night, try to make your bedroom dark and quiet during the day. This helps you sleep better.
