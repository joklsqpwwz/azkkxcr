# Interval Training Q'n'A

Q: Craig you recommend 20 min intervals. If I have extra time, can I do 40 to 45 minutes not including cool down. Would that be as affective? Or would it be a waste of time.

[Interval Training Q'n'A](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Answer: I would like to see people work harder for shorter time periods. If you can do intervals at the same intensity for 40 minutes then you are not working hard enough in the first 20 minutes.

[Interval Training Q'n'A](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/)

You need to ask yourself, "What is redundant in my workout?". Will 5 more minutes of intervals really have a significant effect? Do I really need to do a fourth set of incline dumbell presses? Do I really need 3 different curls to train my biceps?

The answer is almost always "No" and will get you out of the gym faster...but with the same results.

Q: I'm not 100% sure how to do barbell squats. I don't want to wreck my back with bad form. What are the alternatives?

Answer: Single-leg alternative exercises include Pistol squats, Bulgarian Split squats, Forward Lunges, Split Squats, Step-ups, and 1-leg hip extensions (in order of hardest to least difficult).
