# Revolax Tear Trough Filler Training | Intermediate

Preview

About this lesson

This tear trough filler training with Dr. Juan Sopena focuses on safe, gentle correction of under-eye bags using Revolax® Fine. In this step-by-step demonstration, Dr. Sopena shows how to transform visible eye bags into smooth, natural tear troughs - restoring a refreshed and youthful appearance.

The lesson covers how to identify true fat herniation versus volume loss, assess under-eye contours, and inject with precision using a lateral cannula approach. Through micro-aliquot placement and gentle molding, you’ll learn to achieve seamless transitions between the lower eyelid and cheek.

Ideal for intermediate injectors who want to refine their tear trough technique, prevent hollowness, and deliver balanced, natural-looking results with minimal product use.

‍

Things you’ll learn

How to assess under-eye volume loss and determine when filler correction is appropriate.

Safe and precise cannula entry points for lateral access to the tear trough.

Sub-muscular filler placement technique with Revolax® Fine to smooth eye bags naturally.

Step-by-step guidance on product layering, molding, and even skin elevation.

How to harmonize the medial and lateral tear trough areas for smooth, symmetric outcomes.
