What can help alleviate back pain during pregnancy for those with multiple myeloma?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Back pain during pregnancy is common and can often be eased through gentle exercises, stretching, and maintaining proper posture. Supportive footwear, prenatal yoga, and sleeping with a pillow between the knees may also provide relief. However, persistent or severe back pain should never be ignored, as it can sometimes signal underlying conditions such as multiple myeloma or other serious issues. Always consult a healthcare provider for personalized guidance.

During pregnancy, back pain can be common, and there are several ways to help ease it. According to the provided references, engaging in gentle exercises and maintaining good posture can be helpful.

Here are some strategies that may help:

Gentle Exercises: Simple exercises like walking or stretching can help strengthen muscles and reduce pain. Always consult with a healthcare provider before starting any exercise program, especially if you have multiple myeloma.

Proper Posture: Standing and sitting with good posture can reduce strain on your back. Try to keep your back straight and shoulders back.

Supportive Shoes: Wearing comfortable, low-heeled shoes can also help reduce back pain.
