What does it mean if a human vomits bile?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Vomiting bile—a greenish-yellow fluid produced by the liver—can indicate a serious blockage in the digestive system, particularly in newborns, children, and adults with underlying conditions. Common causes include intestinal obstruction, bowel twisting (volvulus), post-surgical complications, or prolonged vomiting on an empty stomach. Symptoms often accompanying bile vomiting include abdominal pain, bloating, constipation, and dehydration. In infants, green vomit is considered a medical emergency and warrants immediate evaluation. Because the causes range from mild to life-threatening, identifying the underlying issue quickly is essential to prevent complications.
