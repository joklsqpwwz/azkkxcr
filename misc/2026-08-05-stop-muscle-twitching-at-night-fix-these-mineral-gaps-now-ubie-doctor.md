Internal Medicine

Yes, mineral imbalances can cause muscle twitching during sleep. Sodium, potassium, calcium, and magnesium are electrolytes essential for proper muscle and nerve function. When levels are too low or too high, nerves may fire irregularly, triggering involuntary twitches, spasms, or cramps—especially at night when the body is at rest.

Because muscle twitching can stem from many causes—dehydration, dietary gaps, stress, medications, or underlying conditions—it's worth pinpointing the source rather than guessing.
