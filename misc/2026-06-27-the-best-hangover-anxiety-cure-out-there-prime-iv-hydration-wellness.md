What Causes Hangover Anxiety?

While researchers do not yet know what the exact cause of hangover anxiety is, there are a few factors that can increase the risk of getting it. The most common reasons are the ways that alcohol affects the brain, dehydration, and the way alcohol affects your sleep.

Although many people around the world drink alcohol as a social lubricant and an ability to stimulate conversation, alcohol is actually a depressant. Since it is a depressant, alcohol can affect the levels of dopamine and serotonin in the brain, which are the neurotransmitters that are responsible for regulating emotion and stabilizing mood.

A night of heavy drinking can initially boost the amount of dopamine and serotonin, which can make you feel happier at first, but the day after will leave you with reduced levels of these feel-good neurotransmitters, which can leave you feeling a bit empty and maybe even a bit depressed. These effects can be especially difficult for those that already have pre-existing anxiety disorders since it can lead to a dangerous cycle of drinking more to self-medicate and dealing with even more severe hangovers and anxiety issues.

Dehydration can also be a big reason for your hangover anxiety. Dehydration is an electrolyte imbalance which occurs during drinking. Alcohol depletes the body of vital minerals.

How Do I Get Rid of Hangover Anxiety?

Having hangover anxiety can feel awful physically, emotionally, and mentally. But you do not have to suffer in silence. Here is what you can do to beat your hangover anxiety.

Replenish Your Body

If you happen to overindulge the night before, make sure you take the time to rehydrate and replenish your body with healthy and nutritious foods. Although you may have heard that a greasy meal is the best cure for a hangover and its symptoms, this can make your stomach upset and can end up making you feel worse, so skip the greasy, fatty food and opt for foods that are rich in protein, fiber, and healthy fats. Drink plenty of water or an electrolyte drink, such as a sports drink, to rehydrate.

Exercise

Exercise is another great way to get over hangover anxiety. You can either get your blood pumping with some cardiovascular or strength training exercises or relax with some yoga or mindfulness meditation. Exercise also helps combat hangover anxiety since it triggers the production and release of dopamine and serotonin, which are the body’s feel-good hormones.
