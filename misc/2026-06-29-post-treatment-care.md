2. Activity and Movement

Resume normal daily activities the same day.

Avoid strenuous exercise, heavy lifting, or intense workouts for 24–48 hours.

[Post-Treatment Care](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/)

Avoid excessive stretching or pressure on the treated area for 48 hours.

3. Massage and Touching the Area

Do not massage or manipulate the treated area unless specifically instructed by your provider.

Avoid tight clothing, shapewear, or compression garments over the area for 24–48 hours, unless otherwise directed.

4. Pain, Swelling, and Bruising Care

Cold compresses may be applied intermittently for the first 24 hours to reduce swelling or discomfort.

Bruising is a temporary and typically subsides within 5-7 days depending on the age and health of a patient. Topical arnica ointment or cream and oral Bromelain supplements will assist in breaking up a bruise to dissipate faster.

Acetaminophen (Tylenol) may be used if needed.

Avoid NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen) and alcohol for 24–48 hours, unless medically necessary.

[Post-Treatment Care](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/blog/2900203700.html)

Sleep with the treated area elevated when possible during the first night.

5. Skin Care

Keep the treated area clean and dry.

Avoid touching the injected areas or using sponges, makeup brushes, etc., which could harbor bacteria and spread infection, for at least 8 hours after treatment.

You may shower the same day unless otherwise instructed.

[Post-Treatment Care](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/)

Avoid hot tubs, saunas, steam rooms, or very hot showers for 24–48 hours.

Avoid exfoliating products, retinoids, or irritating skincare on the treated area for 5–7 days.

6. Sun and Heat Exposure

Avoid excessive sun exposure and heat for 7 days.

Use broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30+ or higher) if the treated area is exposed.

Avoid tanning beds and self-tanners until swelling and redness have resolved.

7. Results Timeline

Hyperdilute Radiesse does not provide immediate volumizing results.

Collagen stimulation develops gradually over 6–12 weeks.

Continued improvement may be seen for several months.

Multiple treatment sessions may be recommended depending on the treatment area and goals.

8. When to Contact the Office

Please contact the clinic immediately if you experience:

Increasing pain, swelling, or redness after the first few days

Signs of infection (warmth, spreading redness, drainage, fever)

Skin color changes, blanching, or severe tenderness

Firm nodules, asymmetry, or concerns that do not improve

9. Follow-Up

Follow-up visits or photos may be scheduled to assess progress

Your provider will advise when additional sessions can be performed if needed.
