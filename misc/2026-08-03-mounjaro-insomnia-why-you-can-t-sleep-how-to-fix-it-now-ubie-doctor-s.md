Doctor's Note: Can't Sleep Since Starting Mounjaro? Why GLP-1s Disrupt Nighttime Rest

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Mounjaro and other GLP-1 medications can disrupt sleep in several ways: gastrointestinal discomfort, shifts in blood sugar and metabolism, and direct effects on brain regions controlling circadian rhythm. Common early fixes include adjusting injection timing, strengthening sleep hygiene, and managing nighttime indigestion. However, persistent insomnia, mood changes, or severe fatigue signal the need for medical evaluation.

Recognizing Insomnia with Mounjaro

Common patterns include:

Difficulty falling asleep even when you feel tired

Frequent nighttime awakenings or restless sleep

Early morning wakening with inability to return to sleep

Daytime fatigue or brain fog despite spending enough time in bed

If these symptoms persist for several nights a week over a month, they meet the medical criteria for insomnia.

Practical Tips to Improve Nighttime Rest

While you work with your healthcare team, try these strategies:

1. Optimize Medication Timing

Administer Mounjaro earlier in the day (e.g., morning or early afternoon) if tolerated.

Avoid late evening dosing, which may heighten alertness at bedtime.

2. Reinforce Sleep Hygiene

Stick to a consistent sleep and wake schedule, even on weekends.

Create a dark, cool, quiet bedroom environment.

Limit screen time at least 60 minutes before bed (blue light can suppress melatonin).

3. Manage GI Discomfort

Eat smaller, balanced meals earlier in the evening.

Avoid heavy, spicy or high-fat foods close to bedtime.

Discuss antacids or other reflux-relief options with your doctor if heartburn is an issue.

When to Talk to Your Doctor

While mild sleep disturbances often improve with these measures, reach out to your healthcare provider if you experience:

Severe insomnia that impacts daily functioning

Mood changes such as depression or intense anxiety

Excessive daytime sleepiness leading to accidents or impaired performance

Any life-threatening symptoms (e.g., chest pain, sudden severe headaches, intense palpitations)

Your doctor can assess whether to adjust your Mounjaro dose, switch medications, or refer you for a formal sleep study.
