2. Regenerative Aesthetic Treatments

Treatments using the body’s own regenerative abilities are growing in popularity. These include:

Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) Uses a patient’s own blood to stimulate collagen production and tissue repair.

Exosome therapy: Uses cell-derived vesicles to promote skin rejuvenation and healing.

Polynucleotides: Derived from fish DNA, these molecules encourage skin regeneration, hydration, and elasticity.

Peptides: Cutting-edge peptides are gaining attention for their regenerative and anti-aging benefits. Key peptides include:

GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide): Focused on skin regeneration, anti-aging, and wound repair.

SNAP-8: A peptide alternative to Botox for reducing the appearance of wrinkles.

Epithalon: A telomerase activator that supports anti-aging, cellular health, and longevity.

MOTS-C: A mitochondrial peptide involved in energy regulation, metabolism, and anti-aging.

FOXO4-DRI: Targets cellular senescence and promotes healthy aging by clearing out senescent cells.

These therapies improve skin texture, boost collagen, and restore volume with minimal downtime. They provide natural-looking results without invasive procedures, making them ideal for patients seeking long-term improvements.

7. Biostimulatory Injectables for Natural Enhancement

Patients are looking for subtle, long-term results rather than traditional fillers. Biostimulation injectables such as:

Sculptra: Stimulates collagen to gradually restore facial volume and smooth wrinkles.

Hyperdilute Radiesse: A diluted form of Radiesse that improves skin quality and elasticity over time.

These treatments stimulate collagen production over time, providing gradual and natural-looking enhancements. Unlike traditional fillers, which provide immediate but temporary volume, biostimulatory injectables improve skin texture and firmness in the long run.
