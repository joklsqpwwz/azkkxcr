Regular testing for STDs is crucial, even if you don't have symptoms. Many infections can go undetected, leading to long-term health problems if left untreated. Getting tested helps protect your health and reduces the risk of spreading infections to others.

How often should I get tested?

The frequency of testing depends on your sexual activity and risk factors. If you're sexually active, especially with new or multiple partners, getting tested regularly (at least once a year) is recommended. If you suspect exposure to an STD, you should get tested as soon as possible.

What are the symptoms of STDs?

Symptoms can vary depending on the infection, but common signs include unusual discharge, painful urination, sores or bumps, and itching. However, many STDs show no symptoms at all, which is why regular testing is important even if you feel healthy.
