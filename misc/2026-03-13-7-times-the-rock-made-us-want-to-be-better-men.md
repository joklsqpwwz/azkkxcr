# 7 Times The Rock Made Us Want to Be Better Men

There are, give or take, about a thousand reasons to want to be more like The Rock. This is the dude that casually crushes early-A.M. workouts on the daily, takes on cheat meals that could feed a small country, films two or three box office hits a year, and, perhaps most miraculously, turned a Jumanji sequel in the year 2018 into a good idea.

But the most inspiring thing about Dwayne Johnson is how despite being the most successful man in Hollywood with about 12 minutes of personal time a day, he still manages to be a really, really good dude. The Rock is a fitness role model in the gym. He's a career role model on-screen. But if we're looking to become better guys in general going forward, it's best to check out what this very large man does for the little people.

Here are seven times The Rock was way too cool of a dude for someone so dang famous.

The day he literally stopped traffic to take a photo

These days, The Rock has more authority than an actual traffic light. That's just the law. "Sometimes traffic is just gonna have to wait to make fans happy," Johnson wrote on Instagram, after an admirer jumped out of his truck to meet him.

"This dude put a grateful smile on my face and got a big ass laugh outta me as well. Besides the traffic jam we caused and his ass almost getting hit by oncoming traffic, making fans like this happy will always be the best part of fame."

When he met a fan holding a giant portrait of his face after a 16-hour work day and was somehow chill about it

Being as cool-headed as the Rock is not something most people can comprehend, much less staying that cool-headed at 1 a.m. after being presented with a giant blown-up photo of your own face.

"Wrapped a 16hr work day and dog tired," Johnson wrote. "As I'm loading up my truck, my security says 'there's a guy up there by railroad tracks who's been waiting for the past 3 nights about 8hrs a night. You're finally gonna see when you drive by. How would you like to handle it?' The only way I know how."

When he bought his mom — and pretty much the rest of his family — a new car

"At 14 I saw my mom crying about our only car being repossessed. This felt good," Johnson wrote in 2012.

And then he bought his uncle a truck.

The time he defied security because he saw a fan crying

"If I see ya crying, I'll call an audible and the game plan changes," The Rock wrote in a 2017 post. Two fans had straight-up snuck past security and into the underground tunnels Johnson uses to get around (that I assume he digs himself with his bare hands), but were so sincere in the attempt, it earned them a conversation with the big man himself.

"Eeeeevry once in a while fans will circumvent layers of security and 'pop up' in areas they're not supposed to be in," the actor wrote. "Security will say 'No' and start to usher them away. Security says no, I see tears and say YES, then I get mascara and tears on my new shirt. Ah f*ck the shirt. Gimme those tears."

When a guy chased down The Rock's car and moved him to tears

Johnson stopped his truck for Nick Miller, who was suffering from Hodgkin's lymphoma.

"I thanked him, hugged him (and his friends), got back in my truck and drove away," the Rampage star wrote. "As I'm drivin' I start shaking my head (and tearing up) at how fragile life is and how amazing and cool the universe was to make this meeting happen between myself and this special kid Nick Miller. Let's always take a moment to count our blessings.. cause there's always something to be grateful for."
