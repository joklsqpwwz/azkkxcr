# 'Bel-Air' Trailer Is the Grittiest Version of 'Fresh Prince' You Didn't Know You Needed

We’re living in an era of reboots, remakes, and reimaginings. And with beloved sitcoms like Full House and One Day At A Time returning to our screens, it was only a matter of time before other faves from the '90s made a comeback. Most recently, The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air got the reboot treatment—but with a key difference.The trailer for Bel-Air, written and directed by filmmaker Morgan Cooper, reimagines Fresh Prince as a dramatic film set in 2019. “Like most 90’s kids, I grew up watching Fresh Prince,” Cooper tweeted on Sunday. “Even as a kid, I always thought there was more to the story… so I decided to make this.”

Cooper started working on Bel-Air six months ago, in collaboration with Sun Squared Media. The opening conflict of the original series, rapped about light-heartedly in the opening credits by Will Smith, is transformed here into a gritty take on gang violence and police brutality which sees young Will (played by Jerry Madison) dodge jail-time by agreeing to move in with hard-ass Uncle Phil.

A lot of familiar characters make appearances: Will’s cousin and sidekick Carlton is revamped as a privileged antagonist who doesn’t want him there; Aunt Vivian is the same voice of support that she was in the original; and the actor playing Jazz could have been lifted directly out of an episode of the original. (Will’s other cousins, Hilary and Ashley Banks, also make brief cameos.)

So far the trailer has racked up more than 970,000 views, with a number of viewers praising Cooper’s vision and tweeting that they want it to be picked up by a studio and turned into a real movie.
