What are the Most Dangerous Steroids?

“Dangerous” is a relative term when it comes to steroids. Each steroid can have specific side effects that pose different risks to different people.

However, some steroids are generally more harmful. Here’s a rundown of some of the worst offenders:

Trenbolone is known as one of the most harmful steroids. It can cause cardiovascular, kidney, brain, and joint damage. Trenbolone was never meant for human use and was only tested in cattle. It is far from being the safest anabolic steroid.

Deca Durabolin isn’t immediately dangerous, but it is often used for bulking when food and salt intake is high. This can spike blood pressure, leading to negative health impacts.

Equipoise (Boldenone) is known as the ‘horse steroid’ for its ability to dramatically increase hematocrit levels. This also raises blood pressure, which can cause significant damage.

Halotestin isn’t much more dangerous than other steroids in terms of physical harm. However, it can make the user quite aggressive, leading to what’s known as “roid rage.”

DNP (2,4-Dinitrophenol) is not a steroid but a fat burner. It can be deadly if used incorrectly. Its risks are extreme, so it’s far from being among the least suppressive steroids.
