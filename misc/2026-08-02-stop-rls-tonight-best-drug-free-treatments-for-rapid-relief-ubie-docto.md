What non-pharmacologic treatments are effective for Restless Legs Syndrome?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Restless Legs Syndrome (RLS) can often be managed without medication. Effective non-drug strategies include regular moderate exercise, leg massages, warm or cool compresses, stretching before bed, and practicing consistent sleep hygiene. Avoiding caffeine, alcohol, and tobacco may also reduce symptom severity.

Because RLS symptoms can overlap with other conditions like nerve issues, iron deficiency, or circulation problems, identifying the true cause matters.
