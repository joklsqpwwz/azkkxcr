Treatment Options:

Medications:

Pain relief can often be managed with medications such as non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) and sometimes stronger pain relievers.

Physical Therapy:

Exercises and stretches can help improve mobility and reduce pain by strengthening the muscles around the affected area.

Surgery:

In severe cases, when other treatments do not help, surgery might be considered to relieve pressure on the nerve.

These treatments aim to reduce pain and improve function. It is important to discuss with a healthcare provider to determine the most appropriate treatment plan for individual needs.
