Why We Chose This

Zinc participates in over 200 enzymatic reactions throughout your body, yet many people don't get enough from diet alone. The bisglycinate chelate form means zinc is bound to an amino acid, allowing your digestive system to absorb it more effectively than conventional zinc supplements. This matters because better absorption translates to meaningful support for immune response, antioxidant activity, and cellular health. The 15 mg dose sits in the range recommended for daily supplementation, making it practical for consistent use without excess.
