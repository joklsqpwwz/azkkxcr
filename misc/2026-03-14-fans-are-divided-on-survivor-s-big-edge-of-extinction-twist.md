We've come to expect the unexpected when it comes to Survivor―blindsides, secret alliances, and immunity idols seemingly pulled out of nowhere. But some of the best twists have come from the game itself, like the Survivor: One World season where both tribes lived on the same beach. This season, fans were thrilled to find out that eliminated players will have the chance to come back into the game after spending some time at an abandoned beach away from the rest of the castaways, called Edge of Extinction. This twist was revealed to the rest of the contestants on last night's episode, and they were understandably shocked.
